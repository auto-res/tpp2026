22nd Theorem Proving and Provers Meeting
{: .lead }

<!-- A meeting for researchers, implementers, and users of theorem proving and proof assistants.
{: .tagline } -->

TPP is held every year since 2005, and provides a forum to exchange ideas for both users and implementors of theorem provers and proof assistants. We aim to make TPP a place where people interested in theorem proving technologies can gather widely.

## Event Information

<ul class="event-list">
  <li><strong>Date:</strong> Monday, November 16, 2026 to Tuesday, November 17, 2026</li>
  <li><strong>Venue:</strong> RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project (RIKEN AIP), Tokyo Office</li>
  <li><strong>Address:</strong> 15F, Nihonbashi 1-chome Mitsui Building, 1-4-1 Nihonbashi, Chuo-ku, Tokyo 103-0027, Japan (<a href="https://www.riken.jp/en/access/tokyo-map/">Access information</a>)</li>
  <li><strong>Co-host:</strong> RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project (RIKEN AIP)</li>
  <li><strong>Organizers:</strong> Sho Sonoda (RIKEN AIP / CyberAgent), Masaya Taniguchi (RIKEN AIP)</li>
  <li><strong>Contact:</strong> <a href="mailto:sho.sonoda@riken.jp">sho.sonoda@riken.jp</a></li>
</ul>
