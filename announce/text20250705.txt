Dear all,

This is Sho Sonoda from RIKEN AIP / CyberAgent.
We are pleased to announce TPP 2026, The 22nd Theorem Proving and Provers Meeting.

The 22nd Theorem Proving and Provers Meeting will be held on November 16-17, 2026 at the RIKEN AIP Tokyo Office in Nihonbashi, Tokyo.

TPP has been held every year since 2005, and provides a forum for exchanging ideas among both users and implementors of theorem provers and proof assistants. The meeting is co-hosted by the RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project.

Event website:
https://auto-res.github.io/tpp2026/

Details on participation and presentation registration will be announced later.
We look forward to welcoming participants interested in theorem proving, proof assistants, formal methods, programming languages, formalization of mathematics, and connections between theorem proving technologies and machine learning or AI.

Best regards,

Sho Sonoda
